# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project welcoming and constructive for
everyone, regardless of experience level, background, identity, or perspective.

## Expected Behavior

- Be respectful and assume good intent.
- Keep feedback specific, technical, and actionable.
- Accept maintainers' decisions when they are made to keep the project healthy.
- Help newcomers understand the project without gatekeeping.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, threats, or personal attacks.
- Discriminatory language or behavior.
- Publishing private information without permission.
- Sustained disruption of issues, pull requests, or discussions.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, close issues, block users, or take
other reasonable action when behavior harms the project or its contributors.

To report a concern, contact the project maintainer privately through GitHub.
Reports will be handled with care and confidentiality where possible.
